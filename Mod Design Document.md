## Mod Design Pillars and Focus

   - Design Statement:
    + The mod will be decently sized casino/card-themed exploration of Mamdani's rise to the mayorship, and Cuomo's fall from relevance, with  alternate events occuring to disrupt the historical result.
   - Design pillars:
	- The mod's writing and theming will be inextricably linked with Casinos and Cards, utilizing color, word choice, images, music, and various other means to conjure a mood of gambling and chance.
	- Competitive, focused elections. The mod will make you focus heavily on attempting to win, with it being challenging for both sides, to emphasize the stakes.
	- The code will be Organized and reusable for future projects, with a very repackagable base. 
	- Remaining accurate in the elections, including the implementation of RCV and other elements, such as the election night timeline.
	- The mod will focus on remaining entirely diegetic during gameplay.
