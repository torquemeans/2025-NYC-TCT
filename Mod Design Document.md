## Gameplay Overview and Systems.


   - Core loop: The player will be presented with a set of questions and select-able answers, which will change and affect their future endings and questions. There will be aqn ancillary card game. The player will Draw and deal certain cards from a deck as they play. The cards they have in their hand can unlock positive answers, or the lack thereof can lock them out of positive answers. 
   - Controls summary: The player will use radio buttons to select answers, and buttons to proceed. There will be an electoral map tab accessible through a button. There will be a trading card deck accessible through a button. There will be a set of game-states accessible through a button.
   - Win/lose conditions: Depends on their answers and answer score effects, as well as their question route.
